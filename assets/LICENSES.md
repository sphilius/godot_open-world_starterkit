# Asset Licenses

Every sourced or generated file under `assets/` has a line here: what it is, where it came
from, its license and any attribution the license asks for. Raw drops in `assets/incoming/`
get their line when they're committed.

| Path | What | Source | License | Attribution |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `assets/characters/*` | Placeholder rigs, meshes and clips (samurai, wolf, grunt, brute, gatekeeper) | Generated by `tools/build_placeholder_rigs.gd` (this repo) | CC0 | — |
| `assets/audio/placeholder/*` | Placeholder SFX, footsteps, UI, wind ambience, combat and boss drum loops, victory sting | Synthesised by `tools/audio/make_placeholder_audio.py` (this repo) | CC0 | — |
